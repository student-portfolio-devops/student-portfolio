# University Student Clubs Portal

A modern, responsive web platform for students to explore, join, and participate in university clubs.

## Overview

The University Student Clubs Portal connects students with campus clubs and activities. Discover clubs aligned with your interests—technology, sports, culture, or debate—and connect with peers to build friendships and develop skills.

Participation Guidelines

Everything you need to know before joining

  • ✓ Must be a currently enrolled university student
  • ✓ Valid student ID card required for registration
  • ✓ Attend at least 70% of club meetings
  • ✓ Annual club fee: PKR 500 (covers events & materials)
  • ✓ Follow university code of conduct during activities
  • ✓ You can join up to 3 clubs per semester
  • ✓ No prior experience needed - beginners welcome!
